# pre-dev

**This folder is a workshop, not a reference.**

Everything here is speculative, exploratory, or aspirational — early thinking about future phases of development that has not been reviewed, approved, or committed to.

- Do not treat any file in this folder as a specification or requirement.
- Do not cite, implement, or make decisions based on anything here during active development.
- Contents are drafts: they may be incomplete, contradictory, or abandoned entirely.

When ideas here mature into real plans, they will be moved into `dev/BACKLOG.md` or a formal spec update. Until then, assume nothing in this folder is authoritative.
